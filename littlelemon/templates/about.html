About Little Lemon

Our story, our passion, our commitment to excellence

Our Story

Little Lemon was founded with a simple vision: to bring the authentic flavors of Mediterranean cuisine to the heart of Chicago. What started as a small family restaurant has grown into a beloved dining destination, known for our commitment to fresh ingredients, traditional recipes, and warm hospitality.

Our Philosophy

We believe that great food starts with great ingredients. That's why we partner with local farmers and suppliers to source the freshest produce, premium meats, and authentic Mediterranean ingredients. Our chefs prepare each dish with care, honoring traditional cooking methods while adding our own creative touches.

Our Commitment

At Little Lemon, we're committed to providing not just a meal, but an experience. From the moment you walk through our doors, you'll be treated like family. Our team is dedicated to ensuring every visit is memorable, whether you're celebrating a special occasion or simply enjoying a night out.
